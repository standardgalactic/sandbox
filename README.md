[Authorization and Its Remainder](https://standardgalactic.github.io/sandbox/authorization_and_its_remainder.pdf)

* [Why Democratizing the Economy Isn't Enough](https://standardgalactic.github.io/sandbox/index.html?track=3)

An examination of economic democracy that distinguishes political authorization from meaningful control over production and its consequences. It argues that voting and formal representation are insufficient without institutions capable of making economic decisions answerable to those affected.

[Mem8 - Ecphoric Reconstruction](https://standardgalactic.github.io/sandbox/mem8-ecphoric-reconstruction.pdf)

* [Why Memory Is A Reconstructive Illusion](https://standardgalactic.github.io/sandbox/index.html?track=2)

A mathematical framework for reconstructive memory that treats remembering as an active process rather than the retrieval of stored representations. It develops resonance-based retrieval, attractor dynamics, temporal encoding, Bayesian inference, and the relationship between forgetting and future adaptability.


[Swype Hero Specifications](https://standardgalactic.github.io/sandbox/SwypeHero-Specification.pdf)

* [The Hidden Geometry of Gesture Typing](https://standardgalactic.github.io/sandbox/index.html?track=1)

A comprehensive technical and design specification for a gesture-driven interaction system. It develops the geometry and interpretation of gestures, input recognition, interface behavior, and implementation architecture, connecting mathematical foundations to practical software design.

[Regularity Without Invariance](https://standardgalactic.github.io/sandbox/regularity-without-invariance.pdf)

* [Why the Global Monsoon Seesaw Breaks](https://standardgalactic.github.io/sandbox/index.html?track=4)

A critical examination of the interhemispheric monsoon dipole and the limits of treating observed climatic regularities as invariant relationships. It distinguishes empirical correlation from structural stability and investigates what can legitimately be inferred from recurring patterns in complex climate systems.
